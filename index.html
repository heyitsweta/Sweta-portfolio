Running 60+ Initiatives at Once

Centralizing Instructor Onboarding

Situation

Instructor onboarding data was scattered across spreadsheets and email threads, leaving Ops, PMs, and HRBPs with no shared view of progress.

Action

Designed and rolled out a single real-time tracker covering 48 onboarding and pre-onboarding instructors, with clear ownership at every handoff.

Impact

Replaced every scattered spreadsheet with one source of truth, giving Ops, Program, and HR full visibility for the first time.

Rebuilding a Disengaged Instructor Network

Situation

Availability signals from instructors were going stale instead of converting into scheduled assignments, and quiet disengagement was going unnoticed.

Action

Ran 150+ individual conversations across geographies and experience levels to surface friction, re-engage lapsed instructors, and unblock scheduling gaps.

Impact

Rebuilt trust with previously disengaged instructors and cut time-to-assign toward a ≤72h target, with several instructors reporting they “felt heard for the first time in months.”

Building a Mentor Community from Zero

Situation

Instructor communication was fragmented across email, Slack, and DMs, creating confusion and inconsistent messaging at scale.

Action

Launched the IK Mentor Community (IMC) as the single hub for updates, pairing a regular posting cadence with direct leadership AMAs to build trust.

Impact

Consolidated all instructor communication into one platform, turning a scattered channel into an active community with a growing base of returning contributors.
